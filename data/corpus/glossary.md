- **Acquirer**: the bank that processes card payments on behalf of a merchant.
- **Journal entry**: a balanced pair of debit and credit records in the [[ledger-service]].
- **Payment intent**: a checkout API object representing a merchant's intention to collect money.
- **Payout**: a transfer of a merchant's available balance to their bank account.
- **Idempotency key**: a client-chosen unique string that makes a write safe to retry; see [[idempotency-keys]].
- **Consumer lag**: how far a consumer group is behind the newest message in a topic; see [[queue-platform]].
- **JWKS**: JSON Web Key Set, the published list of public keys used to verify tokens.
